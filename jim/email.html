Read this first

A stock went up 801% yesterday and is down 48% before the market even opens today. YXT.com — a Chinese online-training software company — closed up 801% Wednesday on no news beyond a routine filing the week before. By 7:30 this morning it had given back nearly half. No announcement explains either move. This is the third time in a week a small Chinese-listed stock has done a one-day moonshot and round-tripped, and the lesson doesn't change: when a stock's entire story is its own price chart, you are not early — you are the exit.

The reverse-split parade is getting crowded. In a single day, at least five small companies filed paperwork to shrink their share counts: iPower (1-for-9, effective tomorrow), SolarMax (1-for-12), Cloudastructure (1-for-30), plus two foreign issuers. A reverse split doesn't change what a business is worth — it's cosmetic surgery to keep a sagging share price above exchange minimums, and it usually precedes new share sales, not recoveries.

One name on the watchlist actually did something. Flash Sports & Media — a small Nasdaq company this scan has tracked since June — signed a term sheet to buy a controlling stake in a Singapore media firm for about $26 million. That's a real proposed transaction with real numbers attached, which makes it rare in this corner of the market. The caveats: a term sheet is not a signed deal, the stock already jumped 25% on the news, and it's fading this morning. Interesting is not the same as investable.

And a real quantum-computing company is coming to market the old-fashioned way. Pasqal, a French quantum hardware firm, filed the definitive merger proxy to go public through a SPAC. Unlike the rebrand-shells that put "quantum" in their name and call it a day, this one has actual laboratories. The history of this scan says the clean SPAC completions — not the name-changes — are where the rare winners have come from. Worth watching into the vote.

Bottom line: still no clean buy. The one real catalyst (Flash Sports) is a non-binding term sheet already up 25%, and everything else filed this week is share-count housekeeping or a pump already deflating.
