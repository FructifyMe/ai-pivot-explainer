Either someone knows something or it's a squeeze; the filing (or absence of one) in the next month settles it.
  • Reverse-split cluster this week — the classic "pre-makeover haircut": Edible Garden (EDBL) 1-for-45 effective July 13 (a chronic diluter — this is survival surgery, not a setup), Nuvve (NVVE) 1-for-18 effective July 1 which then squeezed +64% in a day on the shrunken float, and Baiya International (BIYA) announcing a reverse split by 6-K. Splits themselves aren't buy signals — they're Stage 1 of the costume change at best.
