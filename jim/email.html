  • A company just announced it's becoming an empty shell — on purpose. SOBR Safe, a $3.4M alcohol-detection micro, filed Friday night that it will discontinue ALL revenue operations by July 31 — devices dead, software support terminated, office lease cancelled. The stock's response? +177% Monday. Why would a company shutting itself down nearly triple? Because what's left is the most valuable thing it owns: a clean Nasdaq listing waiting for a new tenant. This is the zombie-shell life cycle caught at frame one — the moment the host dies and the shell goes on the market. Now we watch for the classic follow-ons: a change-of-control filing, a name change, a "strategic pivot." ~$1.12 pre-market today.
  • From Italian football to Solana in one costume change. Brera Holdings — until recently a Dublin-listed owner of European soccer clubs — now trades as Solmate Infrastructure (SLMT), a "Solana-based crypto infrastructure company," and jumped +36% Monday on paperwork that registers previously issued shares for resale. Read that carefully: the filing that fired the move is the one that lets insiders and early investors SELL. The hype and the exit door arrived in the same envelope — same tell as Fast Track last week. ~$6.16 pre, fading.
  • Monday's +416% winner was the boring kind. Twin Vee PowerCats, a $13M boat builder, agreed to be acquired by USFM Corporation — a real merger with a real premium. Not a costume change, no lessons for the playbook except: check the filing before assuming every quadruple is a pump.
  • Fast Track's round trip is completing on schedule. FTRK, Friday's +44% "Sony Music memo + $20M dilution machine" special, closed Monday up another 49% — and is down 26% pre-market this morning. The non-binding-MOU-plus-equity-line combo continues its perfect record of giving it all back.
  • The freeze-dried candy company remains silent. Sow Good (SOWG) enters day 4 of its projected filing window at ~$3.73 — still zero SEC filings since May. Squeeze, not signal, until proven otherwise. About one week left on the model's clock.
  • The brain-tech shell gave some back. Olenox (OLOX), Friday's "shell buys a real business" catalyst name, is ~$4.91 pre-market, −6.6% — the pop faded without follow-through. Watch item: whether real volume ever shows up.
